UNDERSTANDING

PAX SILICA

What is it for?

A U.S.-led initiative to strengthen supply chains for AI and related technologies, including semiconductors > and critical minerals.

Who is it for?

Partner governments and tech industries. According to Philippine officials the proposed hub could bring investment, employment, research and higher-value manufacturing.

How does this concern Philippine territory?

The Philippine proposal identifies 4,000 acres of land in New Clark City, Tarlac, along the Luzon Economic corridor. This site is one part of the wider initiative.

LAND & LIVELIHOOD

Who is given a voice?

CNA reports farmers’ fears about homes, farmland and a lack of information. Aeta communities in particular have protested against the agreement due to potential unlawful displacement and the destruction of their ancestral lands. [3]

OFFICIAL RESPONSE

What did authorities say?

BCDA says the site is outside ancestral domains and plans separate water and power systems. These are agency assurances about a proposed development. [4]

GEOPOLITICAL RIVALRY

Political Tensions Continue To Rise

Journalists and critics alike have criticized the execution of the initiative. Behind the guise of employment, Pax Silica perpetuates geopolitical tensions by countering China's control and power in the AI sector. Aside from the displacement of indigenous communities, many are concerned by its potential effects on national sovereignty. [15]

CIVIC TENSION

THE ASEAN PROTESTS

Aligned with Trump's visit to the Philippines when it hosted the ASEAN Summit, civic tensions were at its peak with protests from citizens and indigenous communities. Numerous violent arrests took place, and while the Pax Silica agreement was not signed as expected, a bill is currently under review to sign the agreement by the end of 2026. [15]
