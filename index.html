FIRST, THE CONTEXT

PAX SILICA

What is it for?

A U.S.-led initiative to strengthen supply chains for AI and related technologies, including semiconductors and critical minerals. [1]

Who is it for?

Partner governments and industries. Philippine officials say the proposed hub could bring investment, research and higher-value manufacturing. [1]

Where does land fit in?

The Philippine proposal identifies about 4,000 acres in New Clark City, Tarlac. This site is one part of the wider initiative. [2]

LAND & LIVELIHOODS

Who gets heard?

CNA reports farmers’ fears about homes, farmland and a lack of information. These are reported concerns, not a confirmed displacement count. [3]

THE OFFICIAL RESPONSE

A different account.

BCDA says the site is outside ancestral domains and plans separate water and power systems. These are agency assurances about a proposed development. [4]

A RESOURCE QUESTION

Water has other users.

Environmental groups question pressure on shared water resources. BCDA says its system will avoid community supplies. [14]

A BENEFITS QUESTION

Jobs need a pathway.

Industry backs the opportunity; experts interviewed by CNA ask how training and local businesses will share the gains. [3]
